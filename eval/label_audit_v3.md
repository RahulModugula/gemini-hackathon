# Label audit v3

Date: 2026-11-21. Scope: `eval/dev_v3.json` (111 claims) and `eval/heldout_v2.json` (43 claims). No model calls were used anywhere.

## Method

1. Every NEW claim was written by hand after reading the PDF words in `build/words/<sheet>.json` (line and row dumps, plus word diffs of each superseded -> current pair from `studio/devset.py`). A claim is built from an evidence selector (a text line, a schedule row, or a single word); `build` fails if the evidence words are not on the sheet, if a word the claim asserts wrongly is in the evidence window, or if an "unclear" claim's tokens occur on ANY current sheet.
2. `bbox_pt` is the padded union of the evidence words (min 320 x 110 pt), clamped to the sheet; each bbox was checked in bounds.
3. The 31 pre-existing dev claims keep their generator labels; they were re-checked here (evidence words in window; stale/decoy tokens) and spot-checked on crops.
4. Visual check: crops were rendered with pymupdf (110 dpi) straight from the PDFs and viewed (column "viewed"); 50 claims were viewed in total (36 dev, 14 held-out).
5. Unclear rule: the asked-for fact must be absent from every current sheet (not just the cited one), so no other sheet can settle it. Claims whose answer turned out to be on another sheet were relabelled or dropped (see change log).
